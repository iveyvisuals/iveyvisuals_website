Hi, I'm Isabella

I started IveyVisuals with a desire to approach design differently. To me, good design has never been just about how something looks, but about the meaning behind it, the story it tells, and the connection it creates. I wanted to create a space where creativity feels collaborative, where ideas are explored with intention, and where every detail reflects the person or brand behind it.

Our imaginations are layered, complex, and uniquely our own. I believe your ideas deserve to be expressed in a way that feels just as authentic. Through thoughtful design and a shared creative process, my goal is to help you communicate what makes your brand yours.

I'd love to hear your story.

The Road to IveyVisuals

My journey to IveyVisuals hasn't followed a conventional path. After graduating from Western University with a degree in Computer Science, I found myself drawn to the possibilities beyond a traditional corporate career. I wanted to build something of my own — a space where I could bring together my technical background, creative instincts, and love for storytelling.

Running IveyVisuals has been a journey of learning, experimenting, and discovering what it means to turn an idea into something tangible. As a one-person creative business, I've had the opportunity to work across web design, graphic design, and content creation, helping businesses express themselves through visuals that feel connected and intentional.

Along the way, IveyVisuals was recognized with two Platinum awards in the 2025 Simcoe County Community Votes awards for Web Design and Graphic Design — a milestone that made the journey all the more meaningful.

Building something from the ground up has taught me that creativity doesn't have to fit into one box. It's about staying curious, embracing the unexpected, and finding new ways to bring ideas together. That's the spirit I bring to every project, and the foundation on which I continue to grow IveyVisuals.
