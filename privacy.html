Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who We Are

This privacy policy applies to IveyVisuals Design Co. ("we", "us", or "our"), a creative services studio based in Barrie, Ontario, Canada. Our website is iveyvisuals.com.

What Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us directly. This includes:

  • Your name, email address, and business name when you fill out our contact form
  • Project details and messages you submit through our inquiry form
  • Booking information when you schedule a call through Calendly

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools on this website. We do not collect any information passively.

How We Use Your Information

Any information you submit is used solely to respond to your inquiry or schedule a discovery call. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses a small number of third-party services that may process data independently:

  • Calendly — used for scheduling calls. When you book through the embedded calendar, Calendly's own privacy policy applies. You can review it at calendly.com/privacy.
  • Google Fonts — our site loads fonts from Google's servers, which may log your IP address as part of a standard web request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic server log data (such as IP addresses) as part of normal hosting operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and email correspondence only as long as needed to complete your project or inquiry. You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

If you are located in Canada or the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at isabella@iveyvisuals.com.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
